FAQ

Common questions.

What's your minimum order quantity?

Standard catalog cells ship from stock with no MOQ. Custom cells require a 500-unit prototype run followed by production tooling at 10,000+.

How do samples and datasheets work?

Datasheets are available on each product detail page. Samples (up to 10 units per SKU) ship within 3 business days on request — start with our inquiry form.

Which certifications do your cells carry?

Our cells are certified to UN 38.3, IEC 62133, IEC 62619, UL 1642, and RoHS. Automotive variants additionally hold IEC 62660 and ECE R100.

Do you support international shipping?

Yes — we ship to 42 countries with full dangerous-goods paperwork (IATA / IMDG / ADR) handled in-house.
