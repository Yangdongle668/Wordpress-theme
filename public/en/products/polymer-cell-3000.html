Why PC-3000

Six advantages, field-proven.

  • High-rate discharge

    15C continuous / 30C peak — the headroom serious motive and UAV loads demand.

  • Safety by layers

    Ceramic separator, flame-retardant electrolyte, and over-current CID protection.

  • Long cycle life

    1,500 cycles to 80% capacity — verified on independent IEC 61960 protocols.

  • Wide temperature

    Full-rated operation from −20 °C to +60 °C without derating penalties.

  • Fully certified

    UN 38.3, IEC 62133, UL 1642, RoHS, and REACH — shipping paperwork included.

  • Drop-in form factor

    Standard 18650 footprint; compatible with existing holders and tab designs.
