Doubling WLTP range
without redesigning the pack.

A drop-in high-nickel chemistry upgrade lifted a production EV platform from 380 to 620 km WLTP — chassis and BMS unchanged, delivery shipped within the model-year refresh window.

Customer
Tier-1 OEM, Germany
Platform
B-segment passenger EV
Pack size
60 kWh → 98 kWh
Timeline
11 months
  • +63% WLTP range (380 → 620 km)
  • 0 Chassis or BMS changes
  • −12% Bill of materials cost
  • 11months Concept to SOP

Challenge

The platform was at the limit of its original chemistry.

The client's passenger EV had been in production for three years on an NMC 622 platform. Competitors were closing the range gap; doing a full re-engineering of the pack — new chassis cutouts, re-homologation, updated BMS — would have cost a model-year and roughly €40M in tooling.

Three constraints framed the brief:

  1. Bolt-compatible with the existing pack tray — no chassis revisions.
  2. Compatible with the existing BMS firmware — no functional-safety re-certification.
  3. Volumetric density uplift of at least 50%.

"We didn't have a year. We had eleven months, and we had to hit the refresh window." — Program Lead, BEV Platform

Solution

A drop-in high-nickel cell with matched external geometry.

We proposed our NMC-811 prismatic platform with a geometry match to the customer's legacy cell — same external dimensions, same mounting pattern, same voltage window. The chemistry change delivered the energy density; the mechanical compatibility preserved the pack architecture.

What we changed

  • Cathode: NMC 622 → NMC 811 (high-nickel)
  • Anode: graphite → silicon-doped graphite composite
  • Separator: upgraded to ceramic-coated (for high-nickel thermal margin)

What stayed the same

  • Cell external dimensions and tab positions
  • Pack tray, cooling plate, and module stackup
  • BMS hardware and firmware (only look-up tables re-parameterized)

Timeline

Eleven months, four gates.

  1. 01

    Weeks 1–6 · Feasibility & cell-level testing

    Chemistry down-selection, cell-level characterization against OEM drive cycles, thermal margin validation.

  2. 02

    Weeks 7–16 · Module & pack-level validation

    Module assembly prototypes, pack-level fit checks, BMS look-up-table re-parameterization and sign-off.

  3. 03

    Weeks 17–32 · Certification & safety testing

    UN 38.3, IEC 62660, ECE R100 recertification deltas. Thermal propagation, nail penetration, overcharge abuse.

  4. 04

    Weeks 33–48 · Ramp & start of production

    First-article inspection on SOP line. Parallel production of legacy + new SKU for 6 weeks, then switchover.

Results

The refresh shipped on time.

The updated pack delivered 620 km WLTP against a 610 km target. First 18 months in production logged zero cell-level warranty returns across 38,000 vehicles. Customer satisfaction on range moved from the platform's historic weak point to its strongest third-party review category.

  • 620 km WLTP (target: 610 km)
  • 0 cell-level warranty returns in first 18 months
  • 38,000+ vehicles shipped on upgraded platform
  • −12% BOM cost versus legacy NMC 622
