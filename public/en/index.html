Engineering

Three advantages, engineered in.

Every cell leaves our line having passed the same three gates — the reason field failures stay vanishingly rare.

  • Safety by design

    Aluminum-laminate pouch, ceramic-coated separator, and flame-retardant electrolyte — layered protection against thermal runaway.

  • Energy density

    High-nickel cathodes paired with silicon-doped anodes push volumetric density 18% above the NMC industry benchmark.

    Electrode coating uniformity under ±1% tolerance — the foundation of 4,000-cycle service life across real-world duty profiles.
