Why join us

What it's like to work here.

The short version: real work, real ownership, fair compensation, and leadership that still walks the factory floor every week.

  • Real ownership

    Engineers own their cells end-to-end — from chemistry decision through customer sign-off. No hand-offs, no silos.

  • Competitive comp

    Top-quartile base salary in every market we hire in, annual performance bonus, and meaningful equity for all engineering roles.

  • Health that travels

    Full family health insurance in every country we operate. Dental, vision, mental health, and an annual check-up on us.

  • Learning budget

    Every engineer gets an annual ¥20,000 (or equivalent) budget for conferences, courses, books — your call, no approval chain.

  • Flexible schedules

    Office roles work hybrid; operations roles work four 10-hour days. Core hours exist to protect deep work, not the other way around.

  • Plant & office mobility

    Internal moves between Shenzhen, Suzhou, Bac Ninh, Fremont, and Munich are funded — relocation, housing, and family support included.

Before you apply

Common questions.

What does your interview process look like?

Four stages: recruiter chat (30 min) → hiring manager interview (60 min) → on-site or virtual loop with 3–4 team members (half-day) → offer conversation. We aim to close the loop within three weeks of initial application.

Do you sponsor visas?

Yes — for R&D and specialist engineering roles we sponsor work visas in every country where we have an office. Relocation support is paid on top of the standard package.

Do you take entry-level candidates?

We do. About 20% of our engineering hires each year are new graduates or career changers. We run a 6-month rotational program through lab, line, and customer-facing work so new engineers see the whole business before specializing.

Can I apply even if my background isn't in batteries?

Yes, and we encourage it for our software, data, and commercial roles. Our best hires in those functions have often come from adjacent industries — we'll teach you what you need to know about cells.

What happens to my résumé if I'm not hired now?

If you opt in during application, we keep it on file for 24 months and reach out when a matching role opens. Otherwise we delete it after 6 months. See our privacy policy for details.
