Before you ask

Common questions.

How fast will I hear back?

One business day, in Asia or EMEA timezones. Americas inquiries typically land early the next Asia morning — usually your afternoon.

Can I visit a plant?

Yes. Plant tours happen with engineering or commercial teams present. Mention "plant tour" in your message and include rough dates.

Do you sign NDAs before the first meeting?

Of course. Send yours with your inquiry, or let us know and we'll send ours.

I'm a student / researcher — can I still email?

Absolutely. Our engineering team responds to technical questions from academia as actively as from industry. No ask is too small.
