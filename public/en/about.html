Established 2004

Twenty years of polymer lithium.

We started as three electrochemistry graduates with a pilot line in Shenzhen. Two decades later, we're still run by engineers — with 1,200 of them across three plants, shipping to 42 countries.

Mission

Energy, safely, everywhere it's needed.

Every product in the modern world is getting mobile — cars, tools, medical devices, sensors, storage. Every one of them needs a battery that's safer, denser, longer-lived than the last generation. That's the work.

We keep it simple: build good cells. Understand the chemistry. Respect the manufacturing. Stay close to the customers using what we make.

Milestones

Two decades, a few turning points.

  1. 2004

    Founded in Shenzhen

    Three electrochemistry graduates, a 500 m² pilot line, and a conviction that polymer lithium was undersupplied.

  2. 2008

    First OEM design win

    Consumer brand picks our pouch cells for a flagship laptop line. Pilot line upgraded to full production.

  3. 2013

    Plant 2 · Suzhou

    Second facility doubles capacity and adds prismatic cell production. First UN 38.3 and IEC 62133 certifications land.

  4. 2017

    Automotive qualified

    IATF 16949 achieved. First tier-1 OEM EV program enters SOP. Energy storage rack product line launches.

  5. 2021

    Plant 3 · Vietnam

    SE Asian facility opens — regional supply for customers who want capacity outside mainland China.

  6. 2024

    Silicon-composite anode in production

    Consumer and EV platforms refresh onto silicon-doped anodes. 18% energy density uplift across the catalog.

How we work

Three things we care about.

  • 01

    Chemistry first.

    We hire electrochemists, and we put them in front of customers. Most battery decisions are chemistry decisions; we don't pretend otherwise.

  • 02

    Respect the process.

    The best chemistry fails without manufacturing discipline. Our plants look boring in tours — that's intentional. Boring processes are reliable ones.

  • 03

    Stay close to the work.

    Leadership walks the line every week. Engineers visit customer plants. It's how a cell maker stays honest about what actually works.
