1. Overview

BatteryCo ("we", "our") is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This policy explains what we collect when you visit our website, contact us, or apply for a job — and what rights you have over that information under GDPR, CCPA, and PIPL.

This policy applies to www.07691688.xyz and every subdomain we operate. It does not cover third-party sites we link to.

2. What we collect

Information you give us directly

  • Contact forms: name, email, company, phone, and the content of your message.
  • Inquiry forms: everything above, plus application details (target voltage, capacity, quantity, timeline) so our sales team can respond usefully.
  • Career applications: your name, email, phone, position applied for, location, LinkedIn profile, résumé, and any cover-letter text.

Information collected automatically

  • Server logs: IP address, user agent, referrer, timestamps, and response codes. Retained 30 days.
  • Cookies: see §8 · Cookies below.

3. How we use your information

  • Respond to your inquiry or application.
  • Ship samples, quotes, or datasheets you've requested.
  • Keep an audit trail of business correspondence.
  • Improve the website (aggregate analytics only — we do not build profiles).
  • Comply with legal obligations (tax, export controls, record-keeping).

We do not sell your personal data, rent it, or share it with advertising networks.

4. Lawful basis for processing (GDPR)

For EU/UK visitors, our lawful basis depends on the activity:

Consent
When you tick the consent checkbox on a form, or opt in to marketing emails.
Contract
When you're our customer or supplier — we process your data to perform the agreement.
Legitimate interests
Responding to your inquiry, keeping our website secure, preventing fraud. Weighed against your rights.
Legal obligation
Tax records, export-control screening, dangerous-goods shipping documentation.

5. Who we share your information with

We share personal data only with a short, audited list of processors:

  • SMTP email provider — delivers form submissions to our inboxes.
  • Cloud hosting provider — runs the website and form service.
  • Accounting / CRM software — used to manage customer relationships once commercial conversations begin.
  • Shipping partners — when we send physical samples or products.
  • Legal / regulatory authorities — only where we are required by law.

Each processor is under contract to use your data only for the services they provide to us, with GDPR-compliant data-processing agreements in place.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have some combination of the following rights over your personal data:

  • Access — ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure — ask us to delete your data (the "right to be forgotten").
  • Restriction — limit how we process your data.
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, where we rely on consent.
  • Lodge a complaint — with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@example.com. We respond within 30 days.

9. International transfers

BatteryCo operates plants in China and Vietnam and offices in the United States and Germany. Your data may be transferred to and processed in any of these locations.

For transfers out of the EU/UK, we use the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses with our processors, plus any additional safeguards required by local law.

10. How we protect your data

  • TLS 1.2+ for all traffic between you and us.
  • Encrypted storage for submitted data and uploaded résumés.
  • Access controls, MFA on admin accounts, quarterly access reviews.
  • Annual penetration tests by an independent security firm.
  • Incident-response playbook with a 72-hour GDPR notification path.

11. Children's privacy
